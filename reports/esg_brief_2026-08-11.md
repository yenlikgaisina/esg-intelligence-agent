## 1. Regulation Watch
*   **EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) Tightens Requirements:** The European Commission has updated its CBAM guidance, confirming that importers of covered goods into the EU must register as authorized declarants from **January 1, 2026**. The first annual declaration, covering 2026 imports, is due by **September 30, 2027**, and CBAM certificates must be purchased from **February 1, 2027**. A 50-tonne annual threshold applies to most goods. There is a strong emphasis on **verified emissions data at the installation level**, with default values incurring mark-ups. This makes granular, verified carbon footprint data for materials like steel and cement from Kazakhstan critical for EU market access and pricing.
    *   **Who it affects:** Kazakhstan steel, cement, and aluminium exporters to the EU, and EU importers.
    *   **Effective date:** Registration from January 1, 2026; certificate purchase from February 1, 2027; first declaration due September 30, 2027.
    *   **Business implication:** Kazakh exporters must rapidly implement systems for accurate, verified, installation-level carbon accounting for products bound for the EU to avoid default penalties and maintain competitive pricing. (Source: Carbon Herald, August 7, 2026: `https://carbonherald.com/european-commission-updates-cbam-guidance-ahead-of-2026-rollout`)
*   **Kazakhstan Construction Code 2026 Implements New Cost Accounting:** The new Construction Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, effective **July 1, 2026**, fundamentally alters how construction companies account for costs. Contractors can no longer use state estimate standards for writing off materials and costs. Instead, they must develop and approve **internal material consumption standards** and use **actual purchase prices** for materials.
    *   **Who it affects:** All construction contractors in Kazakhstan.
    *   **Effective date:** July 1, 2026.
    *   **Business implication:** Construction firms must urgently revise internal accounting policies, develop robust material consumption standards, and implement detailed production accounting to ensure compliance and avoid audit risks. This drives greater internal data transparency which can feed into future ESG reporting. (Source: FCHAIN, July 2026: `https://kz.f-chain.com/en/feautured-post/construction-code-accounting-rules`)
